# E-Commerce Product Performance Analytics (SQL & Python)

An end-to-end data analytics project uncovering platform revenue drivers and operational risks using SQL (DuckDB) and Python (Seaborn/Pandas).

## 🚀 Key Business Insights
* **The Revenue Powerhouses:** Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts are the platform's leading financial drivers, combining for over $2.3M in sales (>4.0★ average). These are prime targets for increased marketing spend.
* **The Churn Risk:** The Bed, Bath, & Table category ranks 3rd in revenue ($1.02M) and 1st in order volume, but holds the lowest average review score at 3.92★, signaling a massive risk of customer churn. 
* **Action Plan:** Recommended a targeted operational audit into product quality and delivery logistics for the Bed, Bath, & Table category to protect a major revenue stream.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Shown
* **Database Layer:** Complex multi-table relational joins, data aggregation, and filtering in SQL.
* **Data Visualization:** Custom dual-axis horizontal bar charting with explicit typography management (`DejaVu Sans`) and qualitative color palettes.
* **Business Acumen:** Translating raw statistical outputs into actionable, jargon-free executive growth strategies.
